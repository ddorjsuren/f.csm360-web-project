Hollowmere Keep
Region
The Sunken Vale
Type
Fortress, dungeon
Recommended level
24 to 30
Controlled by
The Ashen Court
Connects to
Greywater Docks, The Vale Road

Hollowmere Keep is a flooded fortress at the northern edge of the Sunken Vale, built atop the ruins of an older structure that predates the current era. It serves as the primary stronghold of the Ashen Court and is the site of the level 28 story encounter, the Drowned Regent.

The keep is split across three tiers connected by a rotating waterwheel mechanism. Players entering for the first time should expect the outer courtyard to be lightly guarded, with resistance increasing sharply past the second gate.

Layout

The outer courtyard opens onto a flooded causeway leading to the main gate. A side passage on the eastern wall bypasses the first checkpoint but requires the Tideglass Key, obtained from the Greywater Docks quest line.

Notable encounters

  • The Drowned Regent, a tier 2 boss encounter requiring a party of four.
  • Sentry patrols on the second tier, which reset every twenty minutes.
  • An optional puzzle room behind the waterwheel mechanism.

Trivia

  • Hollowmere Keep was added in the Sunken Vale expansion.
  • Its architecture reuses assets from the earlier Greywater Docks region.
